## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a cinematic, high-contrast visual authority tailored for high-end automotive retail in Tierra del Fuego. It reflects the extreme, elemental climate of Río Grande—vast horizons, asphalt cutting through cold terrain, raw industrial strength, and immaculate precision engineering. The aesthetic rejects decorative superficiality in favor of pure form, deep obsidian layers, and cold metallic refractions.

### Aesthetic Persona
- **Architectural & Monochromatic:** Pure structural purity. The environment relies entirely on luminance contrast, pristine geometry, and polished chrome light reflections.
- **Cinematic & Immersive:** Deep field visuals paired with low-reflectance, pitch-dark backdrops that position the vehicles as sculptures of speed and endurance.
- **Extreme Precision:** Micro-hairline borders, razor-sharp technical typography, and engineered structural weight evoke the instrument cluster of a high-performance vehicle.
- **Total Chromatic Discipline:** Strictly void of warm, golden, brass, or saturated chromatic accents. Every surface, accent, state, and interaction is executed within an uncompromising spectrum of obsidian, charcoal, liquid chrome, and stark hyper-white.

## Colors

The palette is engineered entirely out of optical value tiers, industrial metals, and deep light-absorbing foundations. 

### Surface & Atmosphere
- **Obsidian Base (`#050505`):** The absolute foundational background. Absorbs light completely, eliminating viewport boundaries on OLED displays.
- **Void Surface (`#0d0d0d`):** Primary canvas for major sections, split layouts, and modal wrappers.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#141414`):** Default card, tile, and container background. 
- **Sub-Surface Tier (`#1a1b1e`):** Inset panels, active list items, and input wells.

### Structural Metallics & Accents
- **Deep Metallic Charcoal (`#1f2024`):** Structural grid separators and base button containers.
- **Machined Graphite (`#2a2c31`):** Active structural borders, hover surfaces, and technical framework indicators.
- **Cold Chrome (`#848895`):** Secondary editorial typography, meta specs, and subtle tactile highlights.
- **Brushed Platinum (`#b8bac2`):** Elevated icons, active navigation markers, and micro-labels.
- **Hyper White (`#ffffff`):** Uncompromising primary brand mark, display headlines, and high-conversion primary action surfaces.
- **Muted Alabaster (`#e5e7eb`):** High-readability body copy, technical readouts, and secondary buttons.

### Rules of Application
1. **Zero Warmth:** No tints may bleed into amber, yellow, or brass under any circumstances. All cool tints must remain strictly within cold zinc/neutral charcoal balance.
2. **Luminous Contrast:** Primary conversion triggers utilize pure white (`#ffffff`) surfaces with stark black text (`#050505`), generating maximum visual hierarchy over dark obsidian photography backdrops.

## Typography

Typography functions as precision engineering in this system. The interplay between **Space Grotesk** and **Hanken Grotesk** balances mechanical, industrial angularity with effortless readability.

### Type Pairings
- **Space Grotesk (Headlines, Displays, Metadata, Badges):** Modern, sharp, and confident. Its engineered proportions echo the mechanical dials, telemetry instruments, and aerodynamic chassis badges found in contemporary supercars.
- **Hanken Grotesk (Body, Vehicle Descriptions, Financing Terms):** Exceptionally clean and neutral grotesque. Provides zero distraction, letting vehicle specs, mechanical histories, and commercial information read with absolute clarity.

### Typographic Treatments
- **All-Caps Technical Headers:** Labels, vehicle trims, and category indicators should use uppercase setting with expanded letter-spacing (`0.08em` to `0.15em`) to evoke automotive chassis plates.
- **Numerical Dominance:** Power figures (HP), acceleration stats (0-100 km/h), displacement, and price points leverage `headline-xl` or `headline-lg` in Space Grotesk with tabular numerals where possible to maintain cockpit-like readouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture favors grand scale, expansive negative space, and architectural tension, framing each vehicle as an exclusive artifact.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, dynamic gutters of `1.5rem` (`24px`), section edge margins of `4rem` (`64px`) to `6rem` (`96px`). Maximum container constraint fixed at `1440px` for high-end editorial display.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters and `2.5rem` (`40px`) margins.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column fluid grid, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, and compact edge margins of `1.25rem` (`20px`).

### Editorial Rhythm
- **Wide Frame Sections:** Use alternating full-bleed cinematic vehicle viewports followed by contained high-density technical matrices.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Hero car profiles and technical data are paired in asymmetrical 7:5 column relationships rather than standard 6:6 splits, enhancing editorial prestige.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is constructed through cold tonal layering, surface material finishes, and razor-sharp perimeter lines instead of conventional drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Depth Model
1. **Base Ground (Level 0):** Pure `#050505`. Light-swallowing, static foundation.
2. **Machined Slab (Level 1):** Solid `#0e0e11` with a `1px` border of `#1f2024`. Used for inventory vehicle cards, detail tiles, and specs sheets.
3. **Elevated Monolith (Level 2):** `#141416` with a `1px` border of `#2a2c31`. Used for sticky sidebars, floating test-drive booking consoles, and active filters.
4. **Specular Highlight Overlays:** Hover states introduce a cold radial top-edge reflection (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08) 0%, rgba(255, 255, 255, 0) 100%)`) mimicking light glancing off polished auto clearcoat.

### Ambient Shadows & Glows
- Standard drop shadows are discarded.
- Focused focus-rings and active buttons leverage a crisp, cold white luminescence: `0 0 20px -4px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.
- Modals use dark perimeter containment: a solid `1px` border in `#2a2c31` surrounded by a zero-saturation black occluding aura (`0 32px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a razor-sharp, zero-radius geometry (`roundedness: 0`). 

### Rationale & Rules
- **Precision Engineering:** Automotive luxury at its highest caliber is sharp, calculated, and uncompromising. Curves on UI chrome dilute the impact of sculpted vehicular contours in photography.
- **Corner Discipline:** Absolute `0px` border-radius applied across all buttons, inputs, badge tags, vehicle cards, dialogs, and image viewports.
- **Accents:** Angular chamfers (`45deg` corner-cuts of `4px` to `6px`) may be applied sparingly to technical spec tags or inventory stock counter badges to emphasize chassis engineering blueprints.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Conversion / Inquire / Book):** Solid `#ffffff` background with `#050505` text in `action-btn` uppercase. High-contrast, completely flat, instant response. Hover creates a pure luminous bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -2px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`. Active state compresses slightly to `#e5e7eb`.
- **Secondary Action (Configure / View Specs):** Obsidian fill (`#0d0d0d`) with a `1px` border of `#2a2c31` and `#ffffff` text. Hover shifts border color to `#848895` and transitions surface to `#141414`.
- **Ghost Action:** Transparent background, `#848895` text, bordered only on hover with micro underline animation in `#ffffff`.

### Cards & Vehicle Showcases
- **Inventory Card:** Constructed with `#0e0e11` surface and `1px` border in `#1f2024`. Visual container maintains a fixed `16:9` or `3:2` cinematic ratio with `overflow: hidden`.
- **Image Interaction:** Vehicle photography transitions with a slight desaturated tone on idle, blossoming into high-contrast ultra-crisp saturation with a smooth slow-scale zoom (`scale(1.03)`) over 700ms upon card hover.
- **Bottom Bar Specs:** Fixed grid at card base detailing Engine, Transmission, and 0-100 acceleration, separated by crisp `1px` vertical dividers in `#1f2024`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Input Well:** Sharp box with background `#0d0d0d` and a `1px` perimeter of `#1f2024`. Text is `#ffffff` in `body-md`. Placeholder copy sits in `#848895`.
- **Focus State:** Border flashes instantly to `#ffffff` accompanied by a micro inner shadow, maintaining strict architectural sharpness with zero rounded corners.
- **Labels:** Positioned strictly above fields in `label-caps` (`Space Grotesk`, uppercase, tracking `0.15em`, color `#848895`).

### Badges & Technical Indicators
- **Stock Status / Mileage Badge:** Minimalist rectangular chip. Black `#050505` base, `1px` border in `#2a2c31`, text in `#e5e7eb` at `10px` uppercase.
- **Reserved / Exclusive Label:** Solid `#ffffff` block with inverted `#050505` micro-text.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Checkboxes:** Sharp square (`16px x 16px`), `#0d0d0d` surface, `1px` border in `#2a2c31`. When checked, surface snaps to `#ffffff` with a deep `#050505` geometric checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Sharp diamond or nested square styling (no circular elements), preserving the hard-edged aesthetic across state selections.

### Specialized Automotive Components
- **Telemetry HUD Spec Bar:** A modular horizontal component presenting vehicle stats (BHP, Top Speed, Torque, Fuel Economy) stacked vertically: giant numbers in Space Grotesk above uppercase micro-descriptors in brushed platinum.
- **Cinematic Lightbox Gallery:** Full-viewport darkroom modal (`#050505` at 98% opacity) framed by razor-thin chrome controls and an ultra-condensed thumbnail filmstrip at the base.
